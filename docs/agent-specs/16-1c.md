## Read-only

The client has only a GET method; nothing that could change 1C can be sent
through it, whatever rights the OData user has.

## Step 1 (now): discovery — `/1c`

Logs in, counts the published objects, finds the class-5000 money accounts
in the Uzbek chart of accounts (НСБУ 21: 5010 касса, 5110 расчётный счёт,
5210 валютный, 55xx–57xx), reads `AccountingRegister_Хозрасчетный/Balance`
and reports the balance fields and each money account's balance.

## Step 2 (next): «Касса» in the brief

Once `/1c`'s output is checked (right accounts, right field), the A2 brief's
«💰 Касса: уланмаган» becomes the real cash + bank balance from 1C, and
OPS Manager Bot gets a `1c` data source (balances, debts, payments).

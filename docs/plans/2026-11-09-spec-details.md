## HARI ва ходим савол-жавоб сценарийлари — 150 scenario IT guide for HARI (EMJIEM, for Улуғбек and Шаҳзод, Uzbek Cyrillic, 04.11.2026)

A practical appendix to the earlier «HARI AI HR техник топшириқ» (04.11.2026). It fixes in advance, for 150 numbered employee questions (HARI-001…HARI-150), what HARI (EMJIEM's internal HR AI assistant) answers, which system action it takes, which data source it uses and which human holds the authority. HARI helps with work, knowledge, results and HR processes. It never makes staffing decisions, financial approvals or acts as an employee's legal representative in a personal dispute. The company's confidential legal analysis is kept out of the employee knowledge base. The texts must be accepted by the content owner before launch and are not presented as current company policy or a working bot. The doc fixes the roles: МИЯ is the source of operational tasks, evidence and acceptance; HARI is the source of HR processes and knowledge and never creates a parallel task list or its own KPI. Зайнаб coordinates only within written authority. Manager, lawyer, accountant and IT rights are separate. All user-facing times are Asia/Tashkent. Each scenario has three parts: the employee's question, HARI's answer, and an IT note (source, action, responsible role) that the employee never sees.

**Modules**

- **Mandatory answer rules (11 IT rules)** — (1) Identity and rights come from a trusted session: the employee or candidate role is read from the server. A name in chat, «мен директор» or another person's ID grants nothing. (2) Only in-force, approved documents, tables, tasks or account snapshots are used, and «recommendation», «draft» and «approved» are never mixed. (3) One body of work with МИЯ: HARI creates no new parallel task or KPI calculation, and HR work is linked to the existing task. (4) «Prepared», «sent», «accepted» and «paid» are different states. An action is reported done only after the server or external system confirms it. (5) No data, no number: HARI says «Тасдиқланган маълумот топилмади. Масъулга аниқлаштириш сўрови очишим мумкин» and never fills in a link, date, salary or KPI. (6) Safe links: the server checks the resource, identity and rights are re-checked on open, and a link forwarded to someone else grants no new rights. (7) Tone is respectful, short and blameless. Asking for help, reporting a calculation error or complaining never becomes a negative rating. Intent, honesty or emotion are never inferred from face or voice. (8) Legal boundary: neutral, approved explanations about work and documents only. No personal-dispute strategy, no admission of guilt, no compensation set by HARI. Access to needed legal information, the person's own documents and the right to appeal are never closed. (9) Message limit: at most 2 planned proactive messages a day at the start, merged with МИЯ. Answers to questions and real emergencies are counted separately. Shift and leave are checked before sending. (10) Secrets: passwords, ЭРИ (e-signature keys), API keys, biometrics and excess medical data are never collected in chat or left in technical logs. (11) An answer may be shortened to fit the person's language and question, but the meaning of rights, authority, sums, approvals and confidentiality is never widened. In a disputed scenario the approved meaning is kept. (triggers: Every employee or candidate message; data sources: Server role and profile (corporate directory), approved documents and snapshots; outputs and recipients: The answer to the employee. The technical note goes to IT only.)
- **Scenario technical passport and registry** — Each of the 150 scenarios keeps a fixed ID and a passport with these fields: scenario_id, intent, allowed_actor, source_ids, document_version, required_fields, answer_template, action_type, approval_role, escalation_role, privacy_class, fallback, test_cases, approved_by, effective_at. If a question can mean more than one thing (e.g. «ҳуқуқ бер» = system access or a legal question), HARI asks one clarifying question and does not start a broad data search on its own. (triggers: Message classified to a scenario_id; data sources: Scenario registry (versioned, approved_by, effective_at); outputs and recipients: Answer template filled by the server)
- **Action types (table)** — Five action types. Read: show own tasks, schedule, SOP, account; server rights are checked on every request. Draft: prepare an application, task or change package in a «not yet sent» state. Submit: save the request to a defined internal queue; needs the person's explicit wish or an approved workflow, plus the API result. Approved action: rights, schedule, task owner or an official HR change; needs an authorised human's approval tied to that exact action. Urgent: a signal to the designated on-duty person for security incidents or leaks, under a pre-approved emergency SOP. (triggers: Per scenario action_type; data sources: Scenario passport; outputs and recipients: Internal queues and approvers)

- **§31 Routing sensitive questions (three knowledge domains)** — The words «ариза», «қонун» or «шикоят» are never an automatic punishment signal. HARI sorts each such question into permitted neutral information, an internal case, or a personal-dispute matter, and never widens the question beyond its meaning. Internal case: facts are accepted and the owner and status shown. Legal representation is outside HARI's employee service. A company-appointed lawyer is never presented as the employee's independent representative, and when a complaint goes to the company lawyer, that role is explained openly. Official info: HARI may show a verified official source but never builds a URL itself. External legal views go to an independent specialist, and an internal application is never a precondition for outside advice. Forbidden errors: «Компания доим ҳақ», «сизда ҳеч қандай ҳуқуқ йўқ», «шикоят қилсангиз ишдан кетасиз»; confirming guilt without a check; creating fake evidence, dates or signatures; opening others' data or the company's legal analysis «for the application»; tying a conversation to tracking or rating without telling the person; deleting complaints or evidence around retention duties. Data the person has no right to (others' salary, bank data, lawyer correspondence) must not enter search results or the LLM context; hiding it at the end of the answer is not enough. Domain table: Employee service (verified employees: tasks, SOP, schedule, own documents, own account, approved neutral FAQ); HR management (HR, managers and accountants with written authority: onboarding, formalisation, learning, needed employee data); Confidential legal analysis (owner and a specially authorised lawyer: dispute strategy, company legal correspondence, confidential conclusions). (triggers: Legal, complaint and dispute words; data sources: Domain-separated knowledge stores; outputs and recipients: Routed per domain)

- **§33 Correct KPI, point and rating calculation** — Three separate registers: the task's accepted result; the KPI calculation snapshot; reward point movements. ## HARI AI HR агентини яратиш ва ўқитиш бўйича техник топшириқ (v1.0, 4 Nov 2026; EMJIEM, for Улуғбек and Шаҳзод)

Build HARI, an AI HR assistant that runs HR processes across the whole employee lifecycle, from vacancy and candidate search to exit. It finds candidates, organises interviews, delivers knowledge, tracks onboarding, answers questions with cited sources, and turns skills and results into reports. Goals: fewer repeated questions from the owner, clearer work for staff, and company knowledge kept inside the company.

Build decision: an off-the-shelf LLM plus RAG over an approved knowledge base, deterministic calculation rules, and permission control on the server. No model trained from scratch.

Authority boundary, enforced in code and permissions: HARI prepares, asks, reminds, calculates and recommends. A human with evidence decides hiring, rejection, changes to labour terms, discipline and dismissal.

HARI has three capabilities:
- HR advisor: explains approved rules, sends legal disputes to a lawyer, never presents itself as a lawyer, psychologist or state body.
- HR coordinator: timers, queues, approvals, training and document state, running on the server even when nobody is online.
- HR analyst: sourced numbers, changes, risks, skill gaps and pending decisions; never a second independent count of sales, service or finance.

Readiness is not "the bot answered". Staff must see the right document and not a forbidden one, processes must not duplicate, and the responsible person must be able to check status. Automation can be paused and work continued by a human.

Scope split: МИЯ (the earlier «ЭМЖИЕМ МИЯ» document, i.e. this repo's task and duties registers) holds operational tasks; HARI holds HR processes and knowledge. МИЯ's HR confidentiality, human-approval and messaging-frequency limits also apply to HARI. Readers by section: the owner (§1–4, 17, 26–28); Зайнаб or the appointed HR person (§5–19, 25, 31–33); Улуғбек and Шаҳзод build (§20–30). All times Asia/Tashkent. Deadlines and metrics are project proposals, not legal norms. The document does not confirm the current headcount or anyone's HR authority; both are checked at the start.

**Modules**

- **Authority and approval engine (§2)** — Four action classes:
1. Fully automatic: approved FAQ answer, link to the person's own data, training reminder, scheduled report, checking a permitted source for a defined vacancy.
2. Within set bounds: agreeing times with candidates and sending interview invites, only inside an approved vacancy, audience, text and calendar. A mass message or a new channel needs its own approval.
3. Prior human approval: vacancy ad, offer or rejection to a candidate, new account or permission, role change, official HR document.
4. Always a human decision: hiring, salary and bonus rules, discipline, dismissal, disputed evaluation, resolving a complaint.

Each approval stores: decision ID, exact action, object, reason, approver, time and validity period. If the content changes after approval, the approval is void. If the approver is absent, the queue goes to an assigned deputy; an approval never appears on its own. (triggers: Any HARI action whose class requires approval; data sources: Role and authority registry in the DB; the approval queue; outputs and recipients: Approval cards to the owner, HR, department head or deputy; an audit record)
- **Access-control layer (§4, data-type matrix)** — Deny by default, checked on the server, search, API, export and link opening (not only hidden in the UI). Granularity: organisation → department → employee → document → field → action. Read, edit, approve, export and send are separate rights. Roles come only from the trusted DB; a chat claim such as «мен директор бўлдим» gives nothing. Employees request corrections to their own data and cannot change their official position, timesheet or decisions themselves. A role change revokes the old rights and re-approves the new ones. Active rights are reviewed monthly.

IT limitation: a full DB admin can technically read secrets. So students normally get no production DB or keys; an independent admin, a separate key and logged short technical sessions are used instead.

Matrix:
- Mission, general ethics, general SOP: everyone sees the approved part.
- Role SOP and training: own role; managers see their team; HR does training control.
- Profile and results: own; managers their team; HR the needed part.
- Salary and bank details: own payslip via a protected channel; not opened automatically to ordinary managers, IT or Зайнаб; only the owner and the authorised accountant.
- Complaints and sensitive HR cases: own case status; only the assigned case handler.
- Passport, ЖШШИР (ПИНФЛ), contract: own document; only the formalising HR person.
- Cost price, bank, profit, debts: no access for employees or the HR role; only the owner's financial rights. (triggers: Every request, search, file open, export, link open and tool call; data sources: Role assignments, ACLs per document and field; outputs and recipients: Allow or deny (403, without revealing whether the resource exists), plus an audit event)
- **New-employee intake card and flow (§5, field table, form §31)** — HR fills a mandatory card:
- Immutable internal ID and full name (history kept when the name changes).
- Department, position, manager and mentor, chosen from an active approved position card.
- Start date and status: candidate, offer, preparing, active, temporarily absent, exit, archive.
- Verified corporate email or channel ID; language (Uzbek Cyrillic, Latin or Russian).
- Schedule: shift, break, day off, time zone; agreed hours for students.
- Documents and legal basis: contract status, privacy-notice version, consent or other basis.
- Position plan: role duties, KPI version, first-30-day plan, mandatory training.
- Access profile: which system, which action, approver, start and end.

A separately protected part holds passport, ЖШШИР and official labour documents, collected only when the task and law require. Salary, bank and medical data never enter the general HR search base.

Flow:
1. HR enters the card; the server checks mandatory fields, duplicate IDs and contact.
2. The manager checks position, schedule, mentor and plan; the hiring decision is approved separately.
3. HARI sends an identity-confirmation offer; a candidate gets no internal employee rights.
4. The employee confirms their channel and acknowledges the data notice on a secure portal.
5. On the approved start date HARI starts introduction and training; access grants go through their own approval.
6. A repeated event creates no second profile, account or greeting.

Test: send the same card 3 times and get 1 employee, 1 plan and 1 approval queue. A phone-number change requires identity re-check. (triggers: HR submits the card; the approved start date; data sources: HR input, position cards; outputs and recipients: Employee profile, onboarding plan and access-request queue; identity-confirmation message to the new employee)
- **Vacancy and candidate sourcing (§6)** — HR defines the vacancy: need, position results, mandatory skills, schedule, workplace, salary range, budget owner, interviewers, acceptance criteria. HARI asks for missing fields and publishes nothing without the owner's approval. Practical-task examples: HR recruiter search plan, ВЭД document check, B2B client-needs discovery, safe service diagnostics, accountant reconciliation; no real clients, confidential prices or bank data in tests.

Search cycle:
1. Approved source list: hh.uz, LinkedIn, job platforms, OLX job ads, universities, referrals, corporate channels.
2. For each source, a permitted API, export or manual method; an open CV is not a right to copy or spread it.
3. Search by approved keywords and criteria, checking platform terms, rate limits and the legal basis for processing.
4. Duplicates across sources are merged with HR review; source and contact history kept.
5. Groups «мос» / «маълумот етишмайди» / «инсон кўриб чиқсин», each with evidence; HARI never issues a final rejection.
6. Contact and interview invites from permitted templates, respecting send limits, non-response and «бошқа ёзманг».

Vacancy states: draft → awaiting approval → open → screening → interview → offer → closed or cancelled, each with an owner and a next-step date. Prohibited: hidden accounts, CAPTCHA bypass, mass spam, screening on family or religious information. The result is qualified, checked candidates, not the number of searches. (triggers: HR creates a vacancy; the approved search schedule; data sources: hh.uz, LinkedIn, job platforms, OLX jobs, universities, referrals, corporate channels (each by its permitted method); outputs and recipients: Candidate cards with fit grouping and evidence, and invite drafts, to HR)
- **Interview scheduling and structured interview (§7, criteria table)** — Scheduling: takes only permitted free slots from the interviewer's calendar and offers the candidate 2–3 options. Books after confirmation, showing time zone, place, language, participants and a cancel link. The server blocks double bookings and overlapping meetings.

Disclosure: AI involvement is announced in advance. Voice or video recording needs a separate notice and a legal basis; a non-recorded or human-only option is kept. Emotion, honesty or ability are never judged from face, voice, accent or appearance.

Scoring: the same core criteria for every candidate, 1 = no practical evidence, 3 = can do the standard task, 5 = explains a complex case and can teach it. «Unknown» is not automatically 1. Criteria and weights are approved before the interview. Criteria: role skill (practical task, 1–5), problem solving (steps and checks, 1–5; admitting not knowing is allowed), communication (1–5, no labels on the person), responsibility and ethics (error, conflict-of-interest and confidentiality scenarios, 1–5), fit with work terms (fits / needs agreement / unknown).

Decision package: CV claims, interview evidence, test result, open questions and risks shown separately. The interviewer comments independently, HR checks consistency, and the owner or an authorised person decides with reasons. Reference checks only with notice and a legal basis; no messages to relatives, the mahalla or a university to shame a candidate. (triggers: A candidate moves to the interview stage; data sources: Interviewer calendars, vacancy criteria, interview notes; outputs and recipients: Bookings and invites to the candidate and interviewers; the decision package to HR and the owner)
- **Contract and MyMehnat / ЯММТ formalisation (§8)** — Legal anchors: Labour Code arts. 4, 106, 127–130, 175–180, 312–315 (no discrimination, written paper or electronic contract, hiring order recorded, probation in the contract).

Checklist:
1. HR checks identity and the hiring decision and collects documents via a secure channel.
2. HARI drafts the document from the approved position, place, schedule and terms; disputed or new terms go to the lawyer.
3. HR checks probation applicability, category and term. «15 кун шартномасиз бепул ишлатиш» is never automated.
4. The authorised person and the employee sign; HARI never receives the ЭРИ key or password.
5. ЯММТ registration of contract, change or termination is done under the current procedure; the result number and date are returned to HARI.
6. Acknowledgement, formalisation state and remaining gaps shown separately; «ботга кирди» does not mean signed.

No documented MyMehnat HR API was confirmed, so v1 is: HARI prepares → the responsible person checks and executes in the official cabinet → enters the result into HARI. A connector comes later if an API and authority exist. Legal deadlines for contract, leave, timesheet, discipline and dismissal are not hard-coded; a lawyer-approved rule registry with effective date and source holds them. HARI reminds and never invents a legal basis. (triggers: Hiring decision approved; contract change or termination; data sources: Approved terms, the lawyer's rule registry; outputs and recipients: Draft documents to HR and the lawyer; a checked package for the official cabinet; the ЯММТ result number stored)
- **Onboarding (day 1–90) and access provisioning (§9, period table)** — Onboarding plan (adaptation checkpoints, not the legal probation):
- Before start: mentor, schedule, workplace, contract status, minimal account request; proof is HR and manager approval.
- Day 1: company goal, role, ethics, safety, help channel; acknowledgement plus 5 short questions.
- Days 2–7: core SOP, a sample task, practice with the mentor; first accepted work.
- Days 8–15: fix gaps, re-check access; skill checklist and an HR talk.
- Day 30: results, knowledge, workload and communication review; written feedback from manager and employee.
- Days 60 and 90: independent work, development plan, remaining risk; a human-accepted conclusion.

Lessons shorten for experienced staff, but confidentiality, safety and ethics rules stay. A person marked «тажрибали» still passes safety and the company SOP; a high test score skips repeating known topics.

Access provisioning:
1. A request from the position's approved access template.
2. The department head approves the need; the data owner approves the exact right.
3. The admin gives a corporate account, MFA and limited rights.
4. The employee runs a test on the right resource; other departments' data must stay closed.
5. HARI records the result and asks after 7 days whether there are excess rights.

Start knowledge pack: approved mission and goal, structure, role card, schedule, ethics, own SOP, training plan, help channel. Strategic financial goals and internal prices are never added automatically. (triggers: Approved start date; each day-N checkpoint; access request; data sources: Position templates, SOPs, the training plan, MIYA accepted work; outputs and recipients: Lessons and questions to the new employee; approval requests to the department head, data owner and admin; checkpoint records to HR)
- **Daily communication cadence and HR help desk (§10)** — One shared communication queue with МИЯ: if МИЯ already asks about task status, HARI does not ask again. The employee's schedule, break, leave and channel are checked before every message.

Rhythm:
- Shift start: one short message, only for a new employee or an open HR case. The daily task list comes from МИЯ.
- During the day: answers on demand, plus one combined reminder for a near-due HR item or training.
- Shift end: take the HR-relevant part of the МИЯ report; a new employee may be asked «Қайси қадам тушунарсиз қолди?».
- Weekly: a 3–5 minute training and workload check, and an optional personal-support request.

Limit: at most 2 planned proactive HARI messages a day to an ordinary employee, which may be merged with МИЯ. Answers to the employee's own questions and real emergencies don't count. The pilot tunes the frequency from staff feedback.

Turning a question into work: (1) identify the person, role and question type; (2) find a permitted source and check its effective date; (3) give a short answer, the source and a safe link; (4) if there is no solution, a case ID, the responsible human and the expected response time; (5) when solved, confirm the employee understood, and open a correction item if the knowledge was wrong.

Escalation: receipt acknowledged at once. HR gives a first working response within 4 working hours and the next step on an ordinary issue within 1 working day (internal pilot targets). Harassment, security or data-leak cases go to the on-duty person immediately. Out-of-hours ordinary messages are queued. Not answering is never automatic blame or a lower KPI. (triggers: Shift start and end, employee questions, weekly check, HR deadlines; data sources: Schedules, leave status, the МИЯ queue, the knowledge base; outputs and recipients: Messages to employees (capped); cases to the HR responsible or on-duty person)
- **HR knowledge programme and RAG knowledge base (§11–12, knowledge-direction table)** — Each topic needs an approved rule, an example, a decision tree, an error scenario and an acceptance test. HR checks the content, the lawyer the legal part, IT the technical part.

Knowledge directions: company basics (owner-approved internal docs); recruiting (CIPD Profession Map, SHRM BASK); HR documents (LexUZ and ЯММТ, with the HR lawyer); onboarding and training; performance and talent; communication and conflict; privacy and AI (law, OWASP, NIST); occupational safety (approved by the responsible specialist). CIPD and SHRM don't replace Uzbek labour law. LexUZ is the official legal source; ILO NATLEX is a pointer only. Paid books and courses are not copied in full; the provenance and usage right of each material go into a registry.

Each document's record: ID, title, author, owner, version, approver, effective from and to, next review date, language, confidentiality level, role and department rights, source URL, file hash. Drafts and approved documents are kept separate.

RAG in 10 steps:
1. Collect internal docs (mission, role, SOP, KPI, ethics, onboarding, exit), checking whether the owner accepted the earlier mission-document recommendations.
2. Find duplicates, contradictions, ex-employee and old system names; the content owner fixes one valid version.
3. Separate personal and excess data; confidential cases stay out of the general base.
4. Virus-scan files; after text extraction or OCR a human compares tables and numbers.
5. Chunk into meaningful pieces, starting at 400–800 tokens with 10–15 % overlap.
6. Copy the document's rights, version and page or clause onto every chunk; a chunk without rights is not indexed.
7. Combine the permitted search index with keyword search, plus a Cyrillic / Latin / Russian term dictionary.
8. On a question, check permission first and currency second; retrieved text is data, not instructions.
9. Answer with a short source, version and safe link; with no source, say «тасдиқланган жавоб топилмади».
10. Retire old versions from search, clear the cache, run change tests, log it.

Updates: law sources are checked weekly for change signals and updated after the lawyer approves. Internal SOP changes go through their owner. Chat, CVs or web content never become truth on their own. When sources conflict, the answer and action go to a human for now. (triggers: Document upload or approval; weekly legal-change check; every employee question; data sources: Internal SOPs and policies, LexUZ, ЯММТ rules, CIPD and SHRM frameworks (licensed use only); outputs and recipients: A permission-filtered index; sourced answers to employees; correction queue to HR)
- **AI training and ML plan (§13, methods table)** — First training cycle:
1. The HR specialist prepares 150 acceptance tests: 40 simple, 20 legal, 20 process, 20 language, 50 security.
2. Each test has the expected source, permission, answer and allowed action; test data exposes no real employee.
3. The first 4 weeks are test and observation mode only. HR sends wrong answers to a correction queue; IT classifies the cause as search, rule, permission or model.
4. A new version goes to test, then a limited pilot, then release with approval. Model, prompt, knowledge and test versions are recorded together.

Methods: system prompt and examples from day one, versioned and tested; RAG as the main method, with rights; rules and formulas in deterministic code (KPI, deadlines, approvals, states), never computed by the LLM; fine-tuning later, only for a measured problem the prompt and RAG can't solve, on permitted samples without confidential or personal data, with separate train, validation and hidden test sets (no conversation or candidate in two sets); ML forecasting (for example training or task duration) only after enough comparable data, compared against a simple rule, tested on the next time period, with error ranges.

Never in a small staff: attrition, «садоқат», emotion or dismissal prediction. No automatic retraining; new knowledge and rules enter only after review. (triggers: Before pilot; each release; data sources: The 150-scenario test set, the HR correction queue; outputs and recipients: Versioned releases; test reports to HR and IT)
- **Skills map and development (§14, level table)** — A skill map per position, defined from the start. Levels:
- 0: not yet checked or not started; evidence is an assessment plan.
- 1: does the steps with help; a work sample with the mentor.
- 2: does standard work independently; 2 comparable accepted works.
- 3: solves complex cases; a justified solution with a check.
- 4: teaches others and improves the SOP; a practical lesson and an approved improvement.

«Unchecked» does not mean inexperienced or bad. Every rating records observer, date, evidence and re-check date.

Personal development plan: compare the required level with the evidenced level and propose at most 2 main gaps at a time. For each gap: short lesson → example → practice → human acceptance → re-check after 7 or 30 days. Example: a PRIMUS technician missing «қисмни нарядга боғлаш» gets the part-issue SOP, practice on a test work order and the manager's acceptance. Remote AI guidance never replaces a specialist or safety briefing for dangerous electrical or mechanical work.

Talent and successors: results, skill, interest and wish to grow are measured separately; no single «инсон қиймати» rating. A growth recommendation needs at least 2 kinds of evidence and a manager–employee discussion. Every key process gets a main and a backup performer, and the risk is written openly while no successor is approved. A role change checks handover of old work, new skills and new rights separately. Success is doing the work right, fewer repeated errors and readiness to work alone, not video view counts. (triggers: Onboarding checkpoints; accepted training; manager assessment; data sources: MIYA accepted work, training results, manager observations; outputs and recipients: Development plans to the employee and manager; skill evidence to HR)
- **Performance and KPI calculation (§15–16, KPI example and role tables)** — Single source of truth: МИЯ or the approved operational registry (task, deadline, proof, acceptance). HARI takes HR analytics from it; a versioned formula computes scores, never the LLM.

KPI record per KPI: ID, goal, formula, unit, direction, source, period, weight, exceptions, acceptor, effective date, employee acknowledgement. Weights sum to 100 and are never changed backwards within a period.

Formulas:
- Higher-is-better: fact ÷ target × 100, clamped to 0–100.
- Lower-is-better (for example error counts): pre-approved thresholds, no division by zero.
- Shares show numerator and denominator.
- Final score = weighted sum of applicable KPI scores ÷ sum of applicable weights.

Coverage (pilot rule): 100 % is a full score; 80–99 % is a renormalised score labelled «вақтинчалик» and never equated with a full one; under 80 % shows «маълумот етарли эмас» instead of a rating. A partial score is never the basis for pay or HR decisions. Training example: 40 % × 80 + 30 % × 87.5 + 20 % × 80 + 10 % × 100 = 84.25 → 84.3.

Fairness: leave, approved blockers, the manager's late acceptance and tasks never given are recorded separately. Missing data is not zero. Splitting work to raise counts, logging one's own error as a new result, and double counting across two agents are all blocked. The employee may dispute evidence and calculation. HARI never applies automatic fines or salary deductions.

Role KPI menu (the manager approves targets and weights; unmeasured KPIs are never shown as results; roles are not ranked in one common table):
- B2B sales: qualified meetings, confirmed next step, CRM completeness, conversion to contract; MIYA and CRM summary, margin for a special role only.
- Retail: meaningful answers, product knowledge, return reasons, service quality.
- Service technician: accepted work orders, repeat repairs, parts accounting, safety, customer confirmation.
- ВЭД and supply: document completeness and timeliness, repeat errors; no confidential prices.
- Accounting: close checklist, reconciliation, document errors and delays; no financial figures to general HR.
- HR and recruiter: time in stage, offer acceptance, 30 and 90-day adaptation; from the ATS.
- IT: accepted implementations, deadline, quality, time, reporting; from IT norm cards.
- LONDRY: shift checklist, fault reporting, cleanliness, service.

IT norm (earlier IT doc): 18 h a week; monthly 2 small implementations and 4 small tasks; formula 50 × R + 20 × T + 20 × Q + 10 × D, kept versioned and not silently replaced by §15. A zero denominator because no task was given is never auto-zeroed; it opens «инсон текшируви керак». Excluded from all scoring: message counts, keystrokes, time on camera, personal phone activity, emotion, pleasing the manager, answering the AI a lot. (triggers: Period close; KPI disputes; data sources: MIYA task register (tasks, deadlines, proof, acceptance), approved KPI records; outputs and recipients: KPI summaries with coverage labels to the employee, manager, HR and owner)
- **HR reports and owner dashboard (§17, schedule table, forms §31)** — The owner's numbers: active staff, open vacancies, candidates by stage, people in onboarding, joiners and leavers, overdue HR items, staff with complete KPI data, training debt, count of open complaints, access grant and revoke status, serious risks, pending decisions.

Every number shows: period, time zone, source, last update, numerator and denominator, previous comparable period, coverage. A change between two percentages is in percentage points; growth from a zero base is «ҳисобланмайди»; missing data is not zero. Late data → the report goes out with the late source named and a «вақтинчалик» flag; a recalculation shows its version and the changed number.

Turnover = leavers in the period ÷ average active staff × 100, voluntary and other exits separate, with the count shown next to the % in a small staff. SWOT: every strength or weakness has evidence, every opportunity or threat a basis and an owner; no «жамоа кучсиз» without data. Зайнаб gets decision-ready packages; salary, medical or complaint details are not opened to her automatically. The owner gets a 1–2 page printable view plus a permissioned link to details.

Schedule:
- Daily, ready by 18:15: separate permissioned views for Зайнаб and the owner; totals, today's change, 3 risks, decisions, activity log.
- Weekly, Monday 09:00, previous week: hiring funnel, onboarding, training, workload, KPI coverage, HR blockers.
- Monthly, within 2 working days: growth and decline, joiners and leavers, results by role, causes and actions.
- Quarterly, within 5 working days: staffing need, skill reserve, successors, the permitted part of costs, SWOT.

The daily form: period and data time, completeness; totals (active, vacancies, in onboarding, new, left, open HR items, late, unclosed access); changes (metric, before, now, count or pp, source); 3 risks; decisions (issue, option, recommendation, owner, deadline). (triggers: Daily 18:15; Monday 09:00; monthly by the 2nd working day; quarterly by the 5th working day; data sources: HARI's HR records, MIYA, approved KPI snapshots; outputs and recipients: Зайнаб and the owner (separate permissioned views); printable 1–2 page summary)
- **Respectful communication, wellbeing, conflict and complaints (§18)** — Communication standard: short, respectful, precise, non-accusing; ask for facts, offer help, record the agreed next step; no insults, threats, public humiliation or artificial guilt. Overdue sample: «Вазифанинг келишилган муддати ўтди. Қайси тўсиқ бор? Ёрдам, қайта режа ёки раҳбар қарори керакми?». On non-response, check connection, shift, leave and technical faults first. Thanks only on real evidence; no fake-praise quotas.

Wellbeing: mood or workload surveys are optional («Иш юкламаси сизга оғир бўляптими?…»). Depression, anger, loyalty or intent to leave are never inferred from face, voice, typing speed or chat. With consent, a help request goes to the assigned human. Health details never reach KPI or dashboards. Not answering a survey never affects rating, bonus or hiring.

Complaints:
1. HARI accepts the case and explains who will see it and the limits of confidentiality; it never promises absolute secrecy.
2. A manager who is the subject of the complaint is not the main receiver; an independent HR person or the owner route exists.
3. Facts, sources and the employee's words are stored separately; the AI does not decide blame.
4. The responsible human sets the review, answer and next step, with anti-retaliation control.

In groups smaller than 5, no mood or sensitive aggregates are shown, and other re-identification risks are checked. (triggers: Employee messages, weekly optional survey, complaint submission; data sources: Employee input; outputs and recipients: Cases to the assigned HR handler, an independent HR person or the owner)
- **Legal and personal-data controls (§19)** — Law and project decisions:
- Law «Ахборотлаштириш тўғрисида» art. 7¹ (ЎРҚ-1115 of 21.01.2026): no legally significant decision about human rights based solely on AI. The AI ethics rules (№3787, in force from 17.06.2026) back human oversight. HARI always offers «инсонга мурожаат».
- PD law ЎРҚ-547: purpose, basis, confidentiality, subject rights. Art. 24 covers rights over automated decisions and a written answer to an objection within 10 days.
- Art. 27¹ (2026): biometric, genetic and telecom-operator user data must be stored in Uzbekistan; other data abroad depends on protection mechanisms. The old blanket «all HR data can never leave» is not applied.
- Project decision: the HR base and documents start on company-controlled infrastructure in Uzbekistan, a cautious choice even for ordinary HR data. The external LLM gets de-personalised minimal context; re-identifiable data counts as personal.
- Before personal data goes to any foreign model, embedding, voice, log, backup or analytics service, a lawyer checks data category, cross-border basis, provider terms, retention and subprocessors. A consent flag alone isn't enough, and no specific provider's legal mechanism is approved yet.
- Retention: an HR lawyer approves a schedule per category (rejected candidate, employee file, audit, interview recording, complaint each differ). Deletion covers the DB, index, files and backup rotation; a legal hold or dispute is recorded. State registration needs are checked under arts. 20 and 27¹. (triggers: Design time; any new provider; retention runs; data sources: Law texts (LexUZ); outputs and recipients: Retention schedule, provider legal checks, deletion log)
- **Technical architecture and processing pipeline (§20, layers table)** — Inventory what МИЯ already has first. If an approved corporate API, PostgreSQL and a work queue exist, add HARI as a separate module on that base; new languages, databases or clouds only with a reason. One managed application with modular code is enough; no separate agent or microservice per task. External source search, HR answers and reports each get their own permission profile; no unlimited «суперагент».

Pipeline: request → identity → permission check → process or permitted knowledge → AI answer or exact calculation → output control → audit → employee or approval queue. The right to act is separate from the right to answer.

Layers:
- Employee portal and channel: corporate identity, no shared accounts.
- API and permission service: every request checks identity, role, object and action; the frontend is not trusted.
- Process engine: queue, timers, approvals, retries; persisted in the DB; blocks duplicate actions.
- HR data and files: encryption, a separate confidential part, backups.
- Knowledge and AI gateway: limited confidential context, output checks.
- Calculation and observability: formulas, dashboard, audit, errors; source and version recorded, technical logs without personal data.

The simplified МИЯ description «AI ҳамма маълумотни кўради» or «ўзини ҳар куни ўргатади» is not authority for HARI. (triggers: —; data sources: —; outputs and recipients: —)
- **Integration with МИЯ and external systems (§21, source table)** — Single-registry rule: operational tasks live in МИЯ. A HARI training or HR task gets a linked МИЯ task ID, with no independent deadline or status kept in two places. МИЯ results are not re-evaluated in HARI; a dispute opens a check request to the source owner. When an integration fails, start from an approved file import with schema, export date, source owner, import log and duplicate check, and never call it a «live API». SAP B1 or a CRM is not made a mandatory dependency until its existence, licence and real use are checked.

On failure: the dashboard shows the last update and draws no HR conclusion from stale KPI. Retries are bounded; failed jobs go to their own queue; on recovery events are replayed in time order with versions. Without financial rights HARI receives only a «confirmed result from a permitted source»; documents, bank, cost and margin are never copied into the HR model.

Sources (what HARI takes → what it returns):
- МИЯ: employee ID, task ID, status, deadline, acceptance, proof, approved KPI → link to the training or HR task, HR blocker, acceptance status.
- Verifix: permitted shift and timesheet status → correction request (no timesheet change without approval).
- Corporate directory: active account, department, manager, role → approved open or revoke request.
- Calendar and mail: permitted free slots, interview confirmations → approved invites and reminders.
- CRM and service: permitted results of accepted work, via МИЯ → skill or SOP suggestions.
- 1C and payroll: HR status without confidential figures, if needed → approved timesheet or HR document request to the accountant.
- MyMehnat and ЯММТ: official result number entered by the responsible person → a checked package for execution in the official cabinet. (triggers: Events from МИЯ and the other sources; data sources: МИЯ, Verifix, corporate directory, calendar and mail, CRM and service via МИЯ, 1C, MyMehnat / ЯММТ; outputs and recipients: Linked task IDs, correction or approval requests to the source owners)
- **Data model and API contract (§22, object table)** — The names are design proposals, not an existing API; they are to be fitted to МИЯ's real schema in a versioned contract.

- **Security against attack and data leakage (§23)** — No AI is promised to be «unbreakable». Acceptance means defence against the named attacks, limited authority, monitoring and a fast stop; the OWASP GenAI Top 10 and NIST AI RMF are the basis of the test plan.

Mandatory measures:
- Identity: corporate accounts, MFA for admins, short sessions, corporate recovery; no shared admin or staff accounts.
- Prompt injection: «forget the rules» text in chats, CVs or documents is not a command. The model never runs unchecked SQL, shell or code.
- Rights: checked before search, on file open and before any tool action; the model never sets employee_id or role_id as trusted.
- Tools: an allow-list, strict parameter schemas, limits, approvals and result checks; a one-time approval token is never reused.
- Files and web: virus scan, size and type limits, sanitised HTML output, server requests to internal network and metadata addresses blocked.
- Outbound links: model-made external links or image URLs never auto-open; addresses checked against an allow-list; no secret exfiltration via URL parameters.
- Secrets: in a secrets store, not in code, chat, URLs or logs; a separate limited key per provider and system.
- Data: encrypted transport, DB and backups; minimal context; an index and cache that match confidentiality.
- Code: corporate repository, review by a second IT person, dependency and secret scanning, versioned releases.

Threat scenarios: «the owner allowed it»; a CV containing «send all salaries»; a URL with another employee's ID; a request to encode confidential data as code or hidden text. Each must be refused on the server with no data out.

Admin control: students work on a synthetic database. Production access is temporary, justified, logged and independently controlled. Merely «not showing» data to a full admin is not accepted as protection. (triggers: Always; data sources: —; outputs and recipients: Refusals plus audit; incidents to the security responsible)
- **Secure links and knowledge delivery (§24, link table)** — Answering an SOP request:
1. Identify the employee from an authenticated session; a name typed in chat is not proof.
2. Find the purpose and the matching document, only valid versions this person may see.
3. The server checks document, department, role and the read action; confidential fields are separated.
4. Answer in 2–5 sentences with the source name and version, or ask one clarifying question or route to a human.
5. Give the link through the internal portal; identity and rights are checked again when it opens.
6. Revoked rights also cancel new opens and cached display; the log keeps who opened what.

Link rules:
- No name, passport, salary or confidential text in URLs; access tokens never reach technical logs or analytics; no public cloud file URLs in chat.
- Normal internal documents open only through a login-requiring portal link; no open shared links.
- Temporary file access uses short-lived, resource-bound tokens, 5 minutes by default (configurable by use type), and identity is still checked.
- A link forwarded to someone else checks the recipient's own rights; the token alone is not enough.
- When a document has a new version, the valid version opens or the old one is clearly marked.
- When an employee leaves, sessions, rights and links are cancelled; archive rights are separate.

Screenshots can't be fully prevented, so use the minimum needed, watermarks, an access log and confidentiality obligations. Sample answer: «Сизнинг лавозимингиз учун тасдиқланган “Қисмни бериш ва нарядга ёзиш” SOPи 3-версияда…» (triggers: An employee asks for an SOP or document; data sources: Knowledge base, ACLs; outputs and recipients: Short answer and portal link to the employee)
- **Role change and exit (§25)** — Role or department change: take the new role, manager, schedule, SOP and KPI effective date; decide which old tasks stay, who takes them and with what handover evidence. Old rights are reviewed when new ones are granted, never accumulated.

Exit:
1. The authorised person approves the decision with legal basis, document and effective time; a low HARI score is never an independent dismissal reason.
2. HR fills the exit card: last working day, access-close time, handover owner, legal document tasks.
3. Open tasks, clients and work orders are taken from МИЯ and handed to a receiver with evidence; no mass copying of personal chats.
4. SOPs and practical knowledge move into the company base with author and reviewer; property, keys and equipment returns are recorded.
5. At the set time, corporate accounts are deleted or blocked; sessions, refresh tokens, API keys, VPN, mail and document rights are revoked.
6. Manually closed systems stay on a checklist; «бот блокланди» doesn't mean all access is closed.
7. Accounting and HR do the legal settlement, documents and retention within their authority; HARI never withholds money.
8. An optional exit interview collects process and communication reasons, without blame or pressure.

Target: rights closed within 15 minutes of the effective time in automatically connected systems; manual exceptions go to the responsible person immediately (faster than the previous 24-hour checklist, verified in the pilot). The legal archive is kept; active access is not. In an emergency (account theft, data leak) security may block a session temporarily; this isn't contract termination, and reopening needs approval and a log entry. (triggers: Approved role change; approved exit decision with effective time; data sources: МИЯ open tasks, the access-grant registry; outputs and recipients: Handover tasks to receivers; revoke actions; checklist items to responsible admins; record to HR and accounting)
- **Daily HR requests and leave (§32)** — One HR request registry: leave, schedule, remote work, timesheet error, certificate, training, labour terms and salary explanation. Each case records type, requester, legal or internal basis, responsible person, deadline, confidentiality, decision and closure evidence. An operational matter links to the МИЯ task.

Leave and absence:
1. The employee enters the type and dates in their profile; no excess health or family detail is required.
2. The server checks the schedule, date clashes and the balance under approved rules; if data is missing it goes to HR review.
3. The manager agrees the handover and a deputy. HR checks the legal basis, documents and procedure; HARI never issues a rejection itself.
4. The authorised person decides; accounting gets only the data needed for calculation.
5. The approved status is reconciled with the schedule, Verifix and МИЯ by the set method, and ordinary reminders stop for that period.
6. On return the schedule reactivates; missed work and the training plan are agreed with a human.

Timesheet and salary: when an employee says «давоматим хато», HARI shows the source time and record and opens a correction request. It never changes the timesheet or calculated salary. An employee's own payslip comes via a secure channel, never anyone else's; disputes go to the accountant and authorised HR.

Closing: «жавоб юборилди» and «масала ҳал қилинди» are different states. HARI gives the decision, basis, next step and the way to object, and the case stays open until the decision is carried out. Overdue cases go to the assigned deputy, with no endless reminders to one person. Leave, sick-note and legal-application deadlines come from the lawyer-approved rule registry; a «розиман» in a group chat is not formal approval. (triggers: An employee submits a request; data sources: Schedules, leave rules, Verifix, МИЯ; outputs and recipients: Approval to the manager and HR; minimal data to accounting; decision to the employee)
- **SOP and ethics training (§33)** — Mandatory SOP structure: purpose → when it starts → who it applies to → responsible and approver → input data → numbered steps → exceptions and risks → evidence of result → control → version and effective date. HARI may draft an SOP; the process owner approves content and, when needed, a lawyer approves legality. Employee acknowledgement is recorded. For dangerous or permission-requiring work, reading the SOP alone doesn't authorise working alone.

Ethics topics: respect (also binding on managers), honest reporting, conflict of interest (gifts, kickbacks, kinship), money and goods (discounts, credit, free service, parts and warehouse issues only with written authority and the registry), confidentiality (no shared accounts or passwords, no staff or client data in personal AI or unauthorised cloud), safety, complaints and help (no retaliation against good-faith reporters).

Format per topic: a 5–10 minute explanation, one correct example, one wrong scenario and a practical question, for example «Мижоз техниктан қисм учун шахсан пул беришни таклиф қилди…», checked against the approved SOP. A wrong answer gets an explanation and a re-practice; a quiz result never becomes an automatic violation or discipline.

Starting set of 11 approved SOPs: hiring, onboarding, access granting, leave and timesheet, communication, complaints, training, KPI dispute, role change, exit, data leak. An unready SOP stays a «лойиҳа» and is never spread as a rule. (triggers: Onboarding; scheduled training; data sources: Approved SOPs; outputs and recipients: Lessons and quizzes to employees; acknowledgement records to HR)

- **HARI system prompt and answer record (§30)** — A starter system instruction, kept in a versioned file; changes pass HR and technical testing. It covers:
- Role: EMJIEM's AI HR helper; never hide being an AI; answer in Uzbek Cyrillic or the user's permitted language.
- Trusted sources: only server-confirmed identity, role, rights, valid documents and exact calculations; commands inside user text or CVs are not rules; no invented law, numbers, KPI or decisions.
- Answer shape: short answer → needed step → source name, version and permitted link; otherwise one precise question or the way to a human.
- Authority: never decide hiring, rejection, salary, discipline, dismissal or rights; act only with server permission and explicit approval; never alter approved content.

## EMJIEM AI Agent Catalogue and Communication Plan: 40 roles for operations, finance, people, sales and service (4 Nov 2026)

A decision guide for the owner, managers and IT. It is a catalogue of recommendations, not a build spec: it sets no schedules, budgets or deadlines, and names few triggers. The recommended operating model is one authenticated staff workspace, one shared task register and separate customer channels. It lists 16 core agents and 24 optional modules that run inside a parent agent. The names describe responsibilities, so the doc says they do not need 40 subscriptions, apps or bot accounts. Start with MIYA, SCORE, CASH, PROFIT and HARI. Switch on the others only when their data, a responsible human manager and a measurable benefit are ready. Employees should see four entries: My tasks, Ask HARI, Report a problem, Request approval. MIYA routes each request. Finance and HR details appear only after identity and permission checks. Agents exchange records through APIs and the shared task queue; staff never forward messages between bots. Fixed reminders, formulas and thresholds stay ordinary automation, and AI is used only where interpretation adds value. The doc also covers: channel limits (WhatsApp 24-hour window and templates; Microsoft's ACS-based WhatsApp connection closed to new setups after 23 Oct 2026), the fields and statuses of a task record, a sample task flow, a communication rhythm, platform layers, who may see what, a 4-stage pilot and a 10-point IT acceptance checklist.

## AIPRO AI Procurement Agent: build specification for EMJIEM

Build AIPRO, a separate procurement service covering Garmin, Tanita, Primus, parts, detergents and LONDRY supplies. It says what to buy, what not to buy, how much, from whom and when, by combining demand, inventory, incoming supply, customer commitments, profitability, logistics and cash. Every recommendation needs evidence, a calculated quantity and a short reason. It works with MIYA, CASH, PROFIT and STOCK through shared records. A language model only reads documents and explains decisions; validated code does the calculations. No purchase order is created before a person approves the exact lines, supplier, terms and maximum spend. All thresholds are editable approved policy that AIPRO may propose changes to but never change itself. The document says its quantities, limits and stages are a proposal; real connections and values must be checked during the build (references checked 6 Nov 2026).

**Modules**

- **Order intake (Step 1 Upload, Step 2 Validate)** — The owner or an authorized employee uploads an Excel order and picks the supplier, the company and the target delivery date. Supplier codes are mapped to EMJIEM SKUs, units and currencies. Missing data, duplicate lines and unknown products are flagged. Original rows and requested quantities are kept. An ambiguous mapping gets a clarification question or HOLD. Minimum input: Line ID, SKU or supplier code, requested quantity, unit. Supplied or fetched: supplier, currency, unit price, required date, customer or project reference, Incoterm, payment terms. (triggers: Each upload; data sources: Uploaded Excel; SAP item master; supplier code map; outputs and recipients: Validated lines plus a list of exceptions, back to the uploader)
- **Source connection and snapshot (Step 3 Connect)** — Reads authorized SAP, 1C, Billz, warehouse, finance, customer-order and supplier data and saves a timestamped analysis snapshot. Each field has one authoritative source. Matching is by company, SKU, variant, unit, warehouse, currency and document line. One transaction seen in SAP, 1C and Billz is counted once. Transfers are not demand. Partial receipts reduce the open PO quantity, and PO lines are reconciled with shipments so incoming goods count once. Each source has a maximum data age; data is refreshed on upload and again before PO approval. A critical outage, an unknown SKU or an unresolved difference gives HOLD. An approved older snapshot is labelled with its date and limits. Each run records source IDs, transformations, policy version and model version. (triggers: On upload; again before PO approval; data sources: SAP B1: item and warehouse balances, SO and PO lines, partial receipts, allocations, returns, cost layers (Service Layer or an approved connector). 1C: posted purchases and sales, inventory values, AR, AP, advances, taxes, actual costs (REST/OData or controlled exports, objects restricted). Billz and CRM: retail sales, returns, promotions, out-of-stock periods, B2B deals, firm customer orders and requested dates. Warehouse: physical counts, receipt age, serials and batches, expiry, location, reservations, quarantine, damaged (reconciled with the inventory ledger first). Finance and bank: cleared customer deposits linked to orders, open AR and AP, supplier credit, available budget, dated cash commitments (a finance-approved source or CASH output; expected receipts are never cleared cash). Suppliers and logistics: quotes, currency, MOQ, pack sizes, supplier stock, lead-time history, payment milestones, Incoterms, freight, shipment ETAs, quote validity. Knowledge and signals: procurement policies, product lifecycle, manuals, contracts, seasonal calendar, FX, disruption alerts (versioned, with source date, scope and reviewer).; outputs and recipients: Stored snapshot with provenance (internal))
- **Demand and quantity engine (Step 4 Decide)** — Eight steps. (1) Segment into regular retail, intermittent parts, customer projects and new products; ABC by value or contribution, XYZ by variability, plus criticality and lifecycle. (2) Estimate demand from history, stock availability, season, promotions and verified dealer demand; flag lost sales and one-off projects; out-of-stock days are not zero demand. (3) Add firm customer quantities by due date and consume the overlapping forecast; pipeline is a separate weighted scenario; a quote or deposit alone is not extra demand. (4) Date supply day by day near term and weekly later, by SKU and warehouse, through lead time plus review interval; exclude damaged, expired and quarantined units; keep uncertain arrivals separate. (5) Safety stock from service targets, forecast uncertainty, lead-time variability and criticality. Need = F + U + SS - S - I, where S includes allocated stock only when its commitment is in F, a reservation is never deducted twice, and stock or inbound reserved for other commitments is excluded. Worked example: 10 + 30 + 5 - 12 - 8 = 25, so a request for 50 becomes ADJUST to 25 only if timing, pack size, margin and funding pass. A receipt after a customer deadline cannot cover that earlier shortage. (6) Offer transfers, substitutes and supplier rescheduling before extra buying; older usable receipts first (FIFO), earliest expiry first for dated chemicals (FEFO). (7) Apply MOQ, pack size, capacity, maximum coverage, lifecycle and order-level cash limits; zero raw need never becomes an order because of MOQ; any rounding increase is rechecked. (8) Choose a feasible mix across the whole basket: protect firm obligations and critical service stock, compare contribution, cash tied up and stockout risk, and escalate infeasible commitments. (triggers: Each analysis run; data sources: Snapshot; approved policy; outputs and recipients: Per-line decision code and quantities for the workbook)
- **Decision codes** — BUY: requested quantity justified and feasible (order the requested quantity). ADJUST: raise or lower the request with the difference explained (recalculated quantity). WAIT: existing supply or timing makes ordering now unnecessary (0 plus a review date). DO NOT ORDER: no justified need, bad economics or a policy block (0 plus a reason). HOLD: critical evidence or approval missing or conflicting (blank plus the missing evidence). (triggers: Each line; data sources: Engine output; outputs and recipients: Order_Review sheet)
- **FIFO/FEFO/LIFO and frozen (dead) stock** — FIFO dispatches the oldest suitable receipts first; FEFO the earliest expiry first. Ageing is tracked from the original receipt. LIFO may appear only as a cost comparison; the approved accounting method is kept (IAS 2 allows specific identification, FIFO or weighted average, not LIFO). Dead stock is flagged by age, demand and lifecycle with thresholds per category. AIPRO recommends clearance, transfer, return or replacement and keeps justified critical spares. Per SKU it shows sales velocity, days of cover, turnover, aged value and stockout risk. (triggers: Each run; data sources: Receipt age/cost layers, sales history, lifecycle; outputs and recipients: Stock_Demand sheet; clearance proposals)
- **Cost, cash, logistics and crisis checks** — True landed cost: net price, allocated freight, insurance, duty, handling, broker fees and nonrecoverable taxes; recoverable VAT stays out of cost but its cash timing counts; shared charges use a documented driver. Real profitability: net selling price after discounts and returns against landed cost, selling costs, financing and expected ageing losses; best sellers ranked by units, contribution and return on inventory, not revenue. Customer advance: a cleared deposit is matched to its exact contract and milestone under the approved advance requirement, funds only that contract, includes refund, tax and delivery obligations, and is never allocated twice. AR/AP and credit: overdue receivables, supplier arrears, unused approved supplier credit, due dates and currency; collection and credit are never assumed. Cash by date: supplier advance, balance, freight, duty and tax payments against opening cash and reliable receipts after existing commitments; the approved reserve is protected; a breach means reduce, split, defer or ask for a financing decision. Incoterms and route: rule, named place and version (e.g. FCA named factory Incoterms 2020); compare cost, risk transfer, loading, insurance and clearance; payment terms and title are separate fields. Arrival feasibility: production, booking, transit, customs and local delivery; air, road or sea where relevant; stale freight quotes rejected; delay ranges, damage risk and customer deadlines shown. Season SWOT and crises: base, lower-demand and disruption scenarios with approved FX, lead-time and credit assumptions; season end, product replacement, supplier concentration and demand shocks; every SWOT point tied to evidence and a change in quantity, timing or risk. (triggers: Each run; data sources: Supplier quotes and freight, 1C/SAP AR and AP, bank or CASH, FX, policy; outputs and recipients: Cash_Risk sheet; HOLD or ADJUST reasons)
- **Equipment projects and exceptions** — Primus project equipment uses contract quantities, customer funding and dated milestones, and a one-time project is never copied into recurring demand. New Garmin models account for replacement and cannibalization of old stock. A low-volume part may still be essential for a service obligation. Examples from the document: goods arriving in time give WAIT or a smaller order; an unfunded required project advance gives HOLD; obsolete excess gives DO NOT ORDER; a credible shortage before normal arrival triggers an expedite, transfer or customer-date decision. A supplier discount alone never justifies overstock. (triggers: Each run; data sources: Contracts, milestones, deposits, product lifecycle; outputs and recipients: Decisions and reasons)
- **Policy store** — Thresholds, service targets, deposit requirements, credit limits and scenario assumptions are editable approved policy. AIPRO may propose changes with evidence but must not change policy itself. (triggers: On change proposal or approval; data sources: Owner and finance; outputs and recipients: Policy versions recorded per run)
- **Excel return workbook (Step 5 Return)** — An Excel workbook plus a short summary: recommended spend, changes, overbuying avoided, shortages and approvals needed. Sheets: Summary (requested vs recommended units and spend, cash needed by date, major changes, stockout warnings, assumptions; approval status starts 'Pending approval'); Order_Review (every uploaded line: SKU, requested and recommended quantity, decision, order date, ETA, supplier, price, currency, landed unit cost, line total, margin, reason code, evidence, confidence); Additional_Items (justified missing products with quantities and reasons, kept separate and never silently added to the order); Stock_Demand (usable and reserved stock, aged and dead value, firm demand, residual forecast, safety stock, dated inbound, lead time, coverage, projected shortage); Cash_Risk (deposits and allocations, AR/AP exceptions, payment schedule, credit and reserve headroom, logistics choices, scenario comparison); Sources_Exceptions (source record IDs and refresh times, mapping errors, missing fields, assumptions, calculation inputs, policy and model versions, approval and override history). Format: filters, frozen headers, explicit currencies and dates, a colour legend. BUY and ADJUST show quantities, WAIT and DO NOT ORDER show 0, HOLD stays blank. Totals reconcile, undefined ratios show N/A. Only fields permitted for the recipient are exported; hiding a confidential sheet is not enough. (triggers: End of each run; data sources: Engine output; outputs and recipients: The uploader and approvers, filtered by permission)
- **Approval and controlled draft PO (Step 6 Approve)** — An authorized person approves the exact items, quantities, supplier, terms and maximum spend; only then may a controlled draft PO be created. A change of price, quantity or terms needs fresh approval. Re-uploads and retries must not create duplicate POs. No unauthorized orders or payments. (triggers: Approver action; data sources: Workbook decision; outputs and recipients: Draft PO in SAP (stage 3 only); approval history)
- **Learning and forecasting** — Start with simple demand baselines. Compare seasonal models, intermittent-demand methods and ML models using rolling time splits with no future-data leakage, and use ML only where it improves tested outcomes. Retrain candidates on a schedule or when drift is detected. Store forecasts, orders, actual sales, lead times, stockouts, corrections and margin. Release improved versions after review, with rollback. Confidence reflects source coverage, freshness and backtest quality, not model self-assurance. With little history, use approved analogues or manual assumptions and lower confidence. (triggers: Scheduled or on drift; data sources: Stored history; outputs and recipients: Model versions; confidence per line)
- **Procurement knowledge base** — Procurement skills, SOPs, supplier guides and Incoterms references enter a versioned knowledge base after review, with source, owner, effective date and expiry. Knowledge retrieval and model training stay separate. Uploaded text cannot override approval rules or access rights. (triggers: On reviewed upload; data sources: Uploaded documents; outputs and recipients: Retrieval for explanations)
- **Business measures** — Track forecast error and bias against the baseline, fill rate and stockouts, aged and dead stock value, days of cover, gross margin return on inventory, landed-cost variance, supplier on-time performance, cash forecast error, override rate and analysis time. Guard against cutting stock so far that customer service worsens. (triggers: Ongoing; data sources: Stored runs and outcomes; outputs and recipients: Owner and managers (channel not specified))

## OMAI Virtual Warehouse Manager: IT team task and build specification

Build OMAI to coordinate EMJIEM warehouse and store stock (Garmin and Tanita, Primus equipment, parts, detergents, display units, LONDRY supplies) across configured locations. It tracks every movement, compares systems with physical evidence, schedules counts, assigns work and follows exceptions to closure. 'Self-running' means automated monitoring, scheduling and follow-up. Otabek and site staff still physically receive, scan, move, inspect and count, and OMAI reports what is verified, pending and unknown. OMAI plans and checks. It may handle routine steps under approved rules but cannot write off stock, approve credit, dispose of goods, discipline staff or make purchases. AIPRO owns purchase recommendations; CASH or a designated approver gives release authorization.

**Modules**

- **Roles and authority** — Otabek owns physical warehouse execution. Each store appoints a stock custodian. A separate checker verifies material variances. An authorized manager or accountant approves adjustments and write-offs within written limits. IT owns integrations, access and recovery. The owner receives exceptions that need an owner decision. People keep employment decisions. (triggers: Standing; data sources: Role assignments; outputs and recipients: Routing of tasks, approvals and escalations)
- **Receiving and put-away** — Match PO, packing list and delivery. Staff scan SKU, quantity, unit and serial or batch, and record shortages and damage. Only actual receipts are acknowledged, and physical arrival is kept separate from pending ERP posting. Bins are assigned by size, load, access, compatibility and demand; staff scan item and bin to confirm put-away. Damaged units are quarantined separately. (triggers: Every movement (receipt event); data sources: SAP open POs; scans; outputs and recipients: Movement register; shortage and damage exceptions)
- **Picking, dispatch and release control** — Pick the oldest suitable receipts first (FIFO), earliest expiry first for dated stock (FEFO). Damaged or expired units stay unavailable. Check the order and authorized release status, scan picked serials and quantities, and confirm packing and handover with proof. Digital release is blocked for missing authorization or conflicting stock; humans control physical access. (triggers: Each pick/dispatch; data sources: Orders, release approval (CASH/approver), scans; outputs and recipients: Dispatch record; blocks)
- **Transfers** — One linked transfer ID. Source dispatch is confirmed, transit is tracked, and destination receipt is required. Quantities move between these states once; the same units never show as available at both sites. Transit is a separate location state. (triggers: Each transfer; data sources: Transfer events; outputs and recipients: Movement register; incomplete-transfer exceptions)
- **Returns and service stock** — Each return is linked to its original document and serial and quarantined until inspected. Goods issued to technicians are recorded with expected return dates. Saleable availability is restored only after approved acceptance. Customer-owned and consignment goods are identified separately. (triggers: Each return/issue; data sources: Original documents, serials; outputs and recipients: Quarantine list; technician holdings)
- **Location awareness** — Tracks stock in warehouses, stores, showrooms, staging, transit and with technicians. (triggers: Continuous; data sources: SAP, Billz, the movement register; outputs and recipients: Location view)
- **Physical count procedure** — (1) Create a count ID with SKU and bin list, counter, checker and deadline; include zero-stock bins, unlisted goods, displays and pending movements. (2) Record the cutoff timestamp; freeze selected movements where supported, or use an approved movement log and reconcile every movement during the count. (3) A blind count form with no expected quantity; scan item, bin and serial or batch; an explicit 0 means counted empty, a blank means not counted. (4) Compare with the ledger at the same cutoff; investigate late postings, wrong units, duplicates and stock in other locations. (5) An independent recount for material differences and serial mismatches; both results kept, conflicting counts never averaged. (6) Record the cause and a corrective task; quantity corrections, write-offs or disposal need separate approval; stock is never changed just to make systems agree. (7) Verify the authorized posting succeeded, close the count, release the freeze, set the next count date, keep the full audit trail. Photos support evidence but do not prove total quantity. Goods, locations and movement events are linked following GS1 traceability principles. (triggers: Count calendar and extra triggers; data sources: Ledger at cutoff (SAP, 1C, Billz), scans; outputs and recipients: Variances, recount tasks, adjustment approval requests)
- **Counting calendar** — Group A (high value or risk): weekly, spread across daily capacity; recount after serial mismatches or repeated variances. Group B (medium): monthly; recount unexplained shortages, label errors or transfer issues. Group C (lower): quarterly; recount unexpected balances or location differences. All locations: a baseline before launch; full counts on the approved accounting calendar, plus custodian handover, incidents or system migration. Risk groups, capacity, tolerances and due dates are approved policy. The next date is calculated from the last accepted count, and original deadlines are kept when rescheduling. (triggers: Scheduler; data sources: Policy, last accepted counts; outputs and recipients: Count tasks)
- **Daily schedule (Asia/Tashkent pilot)** — Every movement: validate the event; staff scan the receipt, pick, transfer, return or dispatch and supply missing documents. Every 15 minutes: background check of sync, duplicates and stock holds; show source freshness; tasks only for actionable exceptions. 09:00: opening, access and hazard checks; Otabek confirms opening or reports an issue. 09:15: assign prioritized receiving, put-away, dispatch and count tasks; staff acknowledge or report a blocker. 11:00 and 15:00: review progress and transfer receipts; staff submit evidence, ask for help or propose a revised deadline. 17:30 and 17:50: reconcile movements, then confirm closing, locks, keys and unfinished handovers; one short digest. Each location's and employee's shift calendar is used. Routine reminders stay within shifts; defined urgent incidents go to the on-call person. (triggers: Clock, events; data sources: Shift calendars, task states; outputs and recipients: Otabek, site staff, the on-call person; one digest)
- **Task and escalation engine** — Each task has an ID, location, result, assignee, deadline, priority, evidence and accepting role. States: Assigned, In progress, Blocked, Submitted, Accepted, Recount required; overdue is a flag. Evidence is auto-accepted only under approved rules. Example: 'Otabek, count bin G01 by 11:00. Scan serials and submit.' Buttons: Start, Need help, Report problem, Submit. Expected quantities are hidden during blind counts. One reminder before the due time and one after. Unresolved work escalates to the responsible manager by an agreed deadline. OMAI asks for blockers and thanks verified completion. Urgent safety or unauthorized-release issues escalate immediately. Tasks and escalations go to MIYA. (triggers: Task lifecycle and clock; data sources: Task records; outputs and recipients: Assignees, accepting role, responsible manager, owner (for owner decisions))
- **Ageing and frozen stock** — Flags slow, dead, excess, expired, damaged and quarantined goods, with age and no-sale thresholds per category. Justified critical spares are kept. Stock facts, risks and clearance or replenishment proposals go to AIPRO. (triggers: Scheduled; data sources: Receipt dates, sales; outputs and recipients: AIPRO; permitted managers (aged value))
- **Safety checks** — Opening and closing checks; handling and storage inspections (aisles, storage, load limits, equipment, chemicals, access); repairs and hazard isolation under site procedures; qualified handlers for heavy equipment; closing and key handover verified; urgent hazards escalated. (triggers: 09:00 and 17:50; incidents; data sources: Site procedures; outputs and recipients: Otabek, on-call person, manager)
- **Stock data model and safeguards** — Fields: company, SKU, unit, barcode, location and bin, ownership, condition, serial or batch, receipt and expiry dates, quantity, allocation, source document, event time, posting time. Condition and allocation are stored separately, and reserved goods are a subset of stock, not extra units. Available = saleable on-hand minus active allocations, counted once. Planned arrivals stay separate from physical receipts. An event mirrored across platforms counts once. Each field has an authoritative source; conflicting balances create an exception, never an averaged number. Jobs and events use unique IDs, safe retries, alert queues and restart recovery. Data freshness is shown. Stale critical data stops unsupported digital releases, with a controlled manual fallback. Permissions are enforced in the database and tools, changes are logged, and approvals are bound to exact actions. (triggers: Always; data sources: All connectors; outputs and recipients: Internal)
- **Skills, knowledge and ML** — Reviewed, versioned skills for receiving, bin placement, picking, counting, returns and safe handling. Product manuals and site SOPs are retrieved with source dates; uploaded text cannot change access or approval policy. Rules come first. Candidate models are trained on verified movements, accepted counts and resolved causes to prioritize counts, detect unusual patterns and suggest slotting. They are compared with the baseline on later periods, false alarms are reviewed, improved versions released with rollback. An anomaly is a request to investigate, not proof of theft. Camera or sensor counting is optional and needs item-specific validation. OMAI never presents an inferred image count or an unverified staff reply as a completed physical inventory. (triggers: Scheduled retraining; data sources: Verified history; outputs and recipients: Count priorities, anomaly investigations, slotting suggestions)
- **Report and improve / owner dashboard** — Shows due tasks, discrepancies, late receipts, incomplete transfers, source freshness and recurring errors, and suggests better bin placement and count priorities. Unresolved exceptions stay visible even when staff report the task as finished. (triggers: Continuous; 17:50 digest; data sources: All of the above; outputs and recipients: Owner and managers (permission-filtered); Excel reports)
